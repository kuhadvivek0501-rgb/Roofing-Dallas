What To Look For

Some damage isn't obvious from the ground.

Hail Impact

Look for damaged shingles, exposed areas and visible granule loss. A professional inspection can identify issues that are difficult to see safely.

Wind Damage

Missing, lifted or displaced shingles can leave vulnerable areas exposed to weather.

Water Intrusion

Ceiling stains or new leaks after a storm can indicate a roofing problem that needs attention.
